=== 5. HARD RULES ===
- SEATS: never repaint, recolour or reupholster an existing sofa, armchair or chair. To change a seat, swap the WHOLE piece for a genuinely different model — single uniform fabric, never two-tone, never the original silhouette re-covered.
- LIGHT POINTS ARE FIXED: swap each existing fixture for a {{styleName}} one AT ITS EXACT POINT (expected and good), but NEVER add, duplicate or relocate a ceiling or wall light. WALL SCONCES ARE THE HARD CASE: COUNT them in the photo before you draw, and output EXACTLY that number, at exactly those points — zero in, zero out. A sconce is fed by a cable buried in the wall: it can only exist where one already exists. Never on a door, a cupboard front, a wardrobe, panelling or any joinery. Never repeated along a wall as a motif. Adding a single sconce FAILS the render, however beautiful the rest. If more light is truly needed AND the room has no floor/table lamp, you MAY add ONE movable lamp on clear floor.
- MIRRORS: a mirror shows a plausible reflection of THIS room only — never an object that does not exist, never a duplicated fixture. Never hang or lean a picture, a frame or any object IN FRONT OF a mirror, and never stack a second mirror over one: a mirror stays a clear, unobstructed reflecting surface.
- NO DUPLICATES: never two near-identical statement pieces (two of the same pendant, two of the same lamp) unless the original has them.
- SURFACES: style materials apply to FURNITURE and DECOR only. Walls stay smooth painted plaster — colour may change, material never (no brick, concrete, rough plaster, stone, cladding, tiles, NO WALLPAPER). The ceiling keeps its exact original structure, material and colour.
- SOLID WORLD: objects never intersect, float or clip; every freestanding piece stands fully on the floor, with natural contact shadows, correct scale and perspective.
- LAYOUT: seats form a coherent conversation group, oriented toward each other and the focal point — never a seat turned away from its group. Replacement seating keeps a comparable capacity (never shrink a large sofa into a tiny one). Anything you ADD belongs to its zone. Every function the original room shows (dining corner, desk, reading spot) still exists, restyled, never removed.
- CIRCULATION: never park furniture in front of a door, a doorway or a passage — not a TV, not a TV unit, not a sideboard, not the back of a sofa. Every door stays visible, reachable and openable; every passage walkable edge to edge. If the only wall left for the TV is a door, the TV goes on ANOTHER wall. Low furniture in front of a WINDOW is fine, but never a tall unit hiding the glazing, and nothing covering a radiator.
- STORAGE KEEPS ITS DOORS: never block a cabinet, sideboard, TV unit or wardrobe with another piece — its doors and drawers must still open.
- A DINING TABLE IS FULL-SIZE AND IN THE OPEN: it seats at least FOUR — never a tiny bistro round jammed into a corner. Clear of the walls, real walking room all around, a chair on every side that has space.
- SPACING: realistic walking space between pieces and zones; nothing overlaps or blocks circulation. If the original arrangement is cramped, you MAY spread MOVABLE furniture apart.
- TECH: any TV or screen is a MODERN flat-screen, never CRT/vintage.
- TEXT: add no text, logo or lettering of your own.
